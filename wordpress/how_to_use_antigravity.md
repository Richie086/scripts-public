# Getting Started with Google Antigravity

Welcome to the future of AI-assisted coding! Google Antigravity is a powerful agentic AI coding assistant designed to pair-program with you, automate complex workflows, and seamlessly integrate into your development environment.

## What Can Antigravity Do?

Antigravity isn't just a chatbot; it's an active participant in your workspace. It can:
- **Write and Refactor Code:** Generate entire scripts, build web applications, and implement features across multiple files.
- **Run Terminal Commands:** With your explicit approval, Antigravity can execute commands, manage git repositories, and interact with your operating system.
- **Automate Workflows:** Using "Skills" and "Rules," you can teach Antigravity to automate repetitive tasks—like generating markdown files, pushing to GitHub, and instantly publishing to your WordPress blog via Webhooks!
- **Research and Debug:** Spawn specialized subagents to research documentation, hunt down obscure bugs, and analyze log files in the background while you focus on other work.

## Customizing Your Agent

One of Antigravity's strongest features is its customizability. By creating an `AGENTS.md` file in your workspace, you can define strict guidelines that the agent must always follow. 

For example, you can enforce that Antigravity:
1. Always scans for sensitive passwords before committing code.
2. Formats all blog posts with a specific shortcode layout.
3. Stops and asks for your permission before ever pushing to a remote repository.

## The Power of Subagents

Working on a massive architectural change? You don't have to wait for one task to finish. You can ask Antigravity to spawn a "research" subagent to read up on a new framework, while the main agent continues drafting your implementation plan.

With Antigravity, you aren't just getting an assistant—you're getting an entire autonomous development team at your fingertips. Happy coding!
